HOW IT USED TO BE DONE

A camera is placed in the center of the room to capture a single panoramic image of the space.

This creates a 360° view from one fixed position, allowing the buyer to look around from that exact point.

Each panorama still has to be captured from a separate camera position, so the experience is built from a series of individual viewpoints rather than one continuous space.

THIS IS THE ENTIRE 360° EXPERIENCE

This warped image contains the complete visual information for the 360° view. It is still only one flat, two-dimensional panoramic photograph.

The illusion of a 3D experience happens when this exact image is wrapped around a sphere and you are allowed to look around it from the inside.
